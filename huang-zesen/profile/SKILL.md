## Who Is Zesen Huang

**Zesen Huang** (黄泽森) is an early-career solar physicist at UCLA (Marco Velli group) specializing in **MHD turbulence, Alfvén wave physics, and Parker Solar Probe (PSP) data analysis**. He has published 20+ papers (2022–2026), including 6 first-author papers in *ApJ Letters* on topics ranging from the "Two Types of 1/f Range" to the "Alfvénon" — an exact nonlinear MHD solution he introduced in 2025.
