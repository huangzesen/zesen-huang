# Zesen Huang: Biography

## Overview

**Zesen Huang** (黄泽森) is an early-career solar physicist whose research centers on **MHD turbulence in the solar wind**, **Alfvén wave physics**, and **Parker Solar Probe (PSP) data analysis**. He is a member of **Marco Velli's research group at UCLA** and has published 20+ papers in *The Astrophysical Journal*, *ApJ Letters*, *Journal of Geophysical Research*, and arXiv, spanning 2022–2026.

His work has produced six first-author papers, including three in *ApJ Letters* (2024–2026) and two groundbreaking arXiv preprints (2025) introducing the "Alfvénon" model and the reinterpretation of switchbacks. He is recognized for the **"Two Types of 1/f Range"** paper (ApJL 990:L34, 2025), which fundamentally reframed the field's understanding of solar wind turbulence spectra.
