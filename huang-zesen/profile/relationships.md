## Primary Mentor

### Marco Velli — PhD Advisor

**Position:** Professor, Department of Physics and Astronomy, UCLA
**Research Group:** Velli Research Group — Solar Wind, MHD Turbulence, PSP Science
**URL:** https://www-ssl.gps.caltech.edu/people/velli/ (Caltech GPS — Velli is jointly at UCLA and Caltech)

Marco Velli is Huang's primary mentor and the dominant figure in his research career. Velli appears as co-author on virtually every Huang publication — from 2022 wave action to the 2026 5-minute oscillation paper. The relationship shows a clear mentorship trajectory:

- **2022–2023:** Huang as PhD student, Velli as senior co-author
- **2024–2026:** Huang increasingly first author, Velli as co-author (supervisor role)

**Velli's background:** Longstanding leader in solar wind theory, known for the Expanding Box Model (with Grappin and Mangeney), wave-turbulence theory, and PSP science. His research group bridges theory and PSP observations.

**Velli's mentoring style (inferred):** Progressively gives Huang first-author leadership while remaining engaged as co-author. This is consistent with Velli's published mentorship of multiple students through to independence.
