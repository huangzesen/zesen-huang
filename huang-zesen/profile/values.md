## Core Research Values

### 1. Data Primacy

Huang's work is fundamentally **data-driven**. His papers consistently begin with observational gaps and end with what PSP data revealed. The theoretical work (Alfvénon, wave action) is always positioned as motivated by and validated against data.

**Manifestation:** Every paper uses PSP data as the primary evidence. Theoretical papers (wave action, Alfvénon) include observational validation or are explicitly motivated by observational findings.

### 2. Theory as Organizing Principle

Despite being data-driven, Huang's theory work shows a commitment to **theoretical elegance**. The Alfvénon is presented as an *exact* nonlinear MHD solution — not an approximation or numerical result. This suggests a value for theoretical rigor and completeness.

**Manifestation:** Huang seeks exact solutions where possible; numerical simulations are presented as validation, not as the primary result.

### 3. PSP as Transformative Instrument

Huang treats Parker Solar Probe not as just another dataset but as a **transformative instrument** — one that has fundamentally changed what questions can be asked. His framing repeatedly emphasizes PSP's unique capability ("remained elusive even during previous close encounters by PSP").

**Manifestation:** Multiple papers are built around the argument "PSP enabled X, which was previously impossible." This is a rhetorical and epistemic commitment to instrumental progress.

### 4. Classification as Scientific Progress

The "Two Types of 1/f Range" paper reveals a deeper value: **classification precedes understanding**. By distinguishing two types of 1/f spectra, Huang enables more precise theoretical work. This reflects a philosophical commitment to taxonomic rigor.

**Manifestation:** The Gaussianity Scalogram is another taxonomic contribution — a classification tool for solar wind structures.

## Methodology

### Theory-Driven, Data-Validated

Huang's research consistently follows this pattern:
1. Identify an observational puzzle or theoretical gap
2. Build a theoretical model or framework
3. Test against PSP/Solar Orbiter data

**Not purely data-driven** (no blind empiricism); **not purely theoretical** (no untested models published as final results).

### Expanding-Box Model as Physical, Not Passive

Huang's use of the Expanding Box Model (EBM) reflects a value: **the expanding solar wind is not a passive bystander**. The expansion effect shapes turbulence evolution in physically important ways. This is a physical commitment, not just a computational convenience.

**Evidence:** Multiple papers explicitly state that the EBM expansion is "not passive" and drives structural changes in turbulence.

### Quantitative Rigor

Every result is quantified. Key markers:
- Spectral indices: exact values (e.g., −0.61 → −0.94)
- Statistical significance: σ values (6σ for the 5-min oscillation detection)
- Radial distances: exact solar radii

## Views on Specific Debates

### On the 1/f Range and Parker (1958)

Huang's position: Parker's original model remains **partially valid** for slow/mixed wind, but the **fast/Alfvénic type has a different origin** tied to the Alfvén wave cascade itself. He neither dismisses Parker nor accepts him uncritically — he distinguishes.

> *"Parker's 1958 conjecture remains partially valid for the slow/mixed wind type, but the fast/Alfvénic type has a fundamentally different origin tied to the Alfvén wave cascade itself."*

### On Switchback Origins

Huang's position: Multiple proposed mechanisms (S-web, jets, interchange reconnection, Alfvénic outflow) may all produce **similar signatures** because they're all manifestations of **curved open field lines**. The debate is reframed from "which mechanism?" to "what geometry?".

### On the Expanding Solar Wind

Huang's position: Expansion is **not a passive background** — it actively shapes turbulence evolution, drives intermittency enhancement, and affects residual energy. This is a significant theoretical commitment that distinguishes his group's work.
