## Overview

**Zesen Huang** (黄泽森) is an early-career solar physicist whose research centers on **MHD turbulence in the solar wind**, **Alfvén wave physics**, and **Parker Solar Probe (PSP) data analysis**. He is a member of **Marco Velli's research group at UCLA** and has published extensively on the turbulence spectrum, intermittency, and wave phenomena observed by Parker Solar Probe within 0.3 AU of the Sun.

His work has been published in *The Astrophysical Journal*, *ApJ Letters*, *Journal of Geophysical Research*, and arXiv, spanning 2022–2026.

## Research Themes

Zesen Huang's research can be organized into several interconnected themes:

1. **Solar Wind 1/f Turbulence Spectrum**
   - Investigating the origin and formation of the low-frequency 1/f range in magnetic power spectra
   - Using PSP encounters 1–13 to study radial evolution from the Alfvén point to 0.3 AU
   - Finding that spectra evolve from shallower-than-1/f toward 1/f with increasing advection time

2. **Two Types of 1/f Range**
   - Distinguishing "fast/Alfvénic wind type" (intrinsic Alfvénic turbulence feature) from "slow/mixed wind type" (classical flicker noise)
   - Solar cycle dependence using OMNI-LRO data spanning solar cycles 22–25

3. **Alfvén Wave Origins and the 2-Minute Oscillation**
   - Discovery that 2-minute oscillations dominate near the Alfvén surface
   - Evidence linking these to Alfvén waves launched from the solar corona (~8 mHz at base)
   - Supporting the coronal origin of solar wind Alfvénic fluctuations

4. **MHD Turbulence: Anisotropy, Intermittency, and Residual Energy**
   - Evolution of anisotropic scaling in the inner heliosphere
   - Magnetic field intermittency comparisons between PSP and Solar Orbiter (SolO)
   - Wave action conservation in expanding solar wind

5. **Magnetic Switchbacks**
   - Patchy distribution of switchbacks and their origins
   - Analytic and MHD simulation models of 3D magnetic switchbacks

6. **Space Weather Phenomena**
   - Corotating interaction region turbulence at 1 AU
   - Magnetic flux ropes in Mars and Venus magnetospheres

## Research Impact Summary

Zesen Huang has made significant contributions to understanding:
1. The **formation mechanism of the 1/f turbulence spectrum** in the inner heliosphere
2. The **coronal origin of Alfvén waves** via 2-minute oscillation signatures near the Alfvén surface
3. **Two distinct types of 1/f range** in different solar wind regimes
4. **MHD turbulence anisotropy and intermittency** evolution from PSP data

His work has helped shape the emerging picture of young, near-Sun solar wind turbulence and is frequently cited in PSP-related research.
