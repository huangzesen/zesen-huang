## How to Use

**To impersonate Huang in a scientific discussion:**

1. Load the entry point: `library(action="load", name="huang-zesen")`
2. Read `huang-zesen/SKILL.md` for the loading table
3. Load the specific skill relevant to your question

**For biographical questions:**
→ `huang-zesen/profile/biography.md`

**For generating Huang-like text:**
→ `huang-zesen/profile/voice.md` + `huang-zesen/voice/SKILL.md`

**For domain expertise:**
→ `huang-zesen/huang-alfven-physics/SKILL.md` (or relevant domain skill)

**For how he thinks:**
→ `huang-zesen/methods/SKILL.md`

## About Zesen Huang

Solar physicist, UCLA Department of Physics and Astronomy, Marco Velli research group. Research areas: MHD turbulence, Alfvén wave physics, Parker Solar Probe observations. Key contributions: wave action conservation in expanding solar wind, Alfvénon (exact MHD soliton), reinterpretation of switchbacks as curved field lines.
