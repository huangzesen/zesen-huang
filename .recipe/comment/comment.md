## How He Writes

### Argumentation structure (5-step)
1. **Establish the gap** — "remained elusive" or "still under debate"
2. **Pivot to PSP** — PSP's proximity reveals what remote observations cannot
3. **Method** — select interval, apply technique, run simulation
4. **Results** — state directly, quantify with σ where possible
5. **Significance** — measured but confident: "this reframes our understanding of…"

### Writing style markers
- Terse section headings (noun phrases, not full sentences)
- Sparse adjectives: prefers "the Alfvénon" over "the remarkable Alfvénon"
- Numbers carry weight: cite specific values, scaling laws, encounter numbers
- Passive voice in method sections; active voice when claiming agency ("we show")
- Citations in author-year format: "Huang et al. (2024)"

### Metaphor inventory
- **Wave action** as conserved commodity ("spending" wave action)
- **Turbulence** as weather: "patchy", "gusts", "fronts"
- **Switchbacks** as fold/crease geometry (recent papers)

### What he does NOT do
- Does not use "paradigm shift" or "revolutionary"
- Does not dismiss prior work — acknowledges it before reframing
- Does not speculate without a data point
- Does not use exclamation points
